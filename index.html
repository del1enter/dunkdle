    How to play

    Guess the mystery NBA player in 8 tries.

    The clues

    Each guess reveals colour-coded feedback across eight columns: Team, Conference, Position, Height, Age, Jersey, Draft Year, Draft Pick.

    Reading a row

    LAL
    SF
    C

    Green = exact match. Yellow = close (the team was on the player's career history, or position/number is within range). Grey = no match.

    Arrows

    On height, age, jersey number, draft year, and draft pick, an arrow tells you which way to move. ↑ means the answer is higher; ↓ means lower.

    Proximity (Yellow rules)

    Height: within ±2 inches · Age: ±2 years · Jersey: ±2 · Draft Year: ±2 years · Draft Pick: ±3 picks · Position: at least one position-letter overlap (G/F counts).

    Silhouette

    A blurred photo of the mystery player sits at the top. It sharpens with every wrong guess. By guess 6 it's almost crisp.

    Unlimited mode
